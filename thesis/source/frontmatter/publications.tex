  \textit{Statistical Mechanics of the $N$-Queens Problem}.
  arXiv:2605.10326 [cond-mat.stat-mech], 2026.\\
  \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10326}{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10326}
